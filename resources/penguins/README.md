# Penguin sprites

`walk/1.png` through `walk/8.png` are the complete side-walk frames from
`Walk (3)/Color` in the supplied "Browser Games - Club Penguin - Playable
Characters - Penguin" download. They are bundled as Qt resources, so installed
apps do not depend on the original Documents folder.

The renderer mirrors these frames for rightward movement and recolors their
blue plumage while keeping the original feet, beak, belly and shading.

Club Penguin characters and artwork belong to their respective owners. These
assets are separate from the application's source code licensing.
